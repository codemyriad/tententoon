Single generated hero picture with an ornate portal collar

Inspired by the top community contest winners (The Astronomer’s Teapot, Before the shutter, and Laundry chute in sage tiles), this single image pairs a square sage-and-cream tile grid and four compass landmarks with a richly detailed celestial majolica, brass clockwork, and botanical wreath rim around the central portal.

Because log(z − c) stretches radii near the portal boundary, that ornate collar fills most of the unrolled strip (panel 1) and winds into the main body of the nautilus spiral (panel 3).
